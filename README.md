### 🚀 About Me & Karvion Labs

Hi, I'm **Abdullah Karif Shahjada**, the founder and lead engineer behind **Karvion Labs**. I specialize in designing autonomous workflows, intelligent AI integrations, and industrial-grade **Test Automation Frameworks (SQA)** that save hundreds of human hours and guarantee rock-solid software quality.

- 🏢 **Organization:** Founder & Lead at **Karvion Labs**
- 🎓 **Specialized Training:** Pursuing an intensive 3-Month Professional SQA & Test Automation Program at **RSN Foundation**
- 🔭 **Current Focus:** End-to-End Web Automation, Robust Regression Suites, API Performance & Contract Testing
- 🌐 **Live Ecosystem:** Creator of **[Karvion BD](https://karvion-bd-five.vercel.app)** — An AI-augmented digital service portal
- ⚡ **Core Philosophy:** *"If a task repeats more than twice, automate it."*
